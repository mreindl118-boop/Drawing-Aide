# Third-party notices

## MakeHuman assets (CC0 1.0)

The figure mesh, morph targets, and rig data bundled in `src/anatomy/mh/`
are converted from the [MakeHuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman)
project's data files, which the MakeHuman Community explicitly released
under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
in September 2020. See `scripts/build-mh-bundle.mjs` for the conversion.

Thank you to the MakeHuman Community for making a professionally sculpted,
riggable human base mesh freely available.
